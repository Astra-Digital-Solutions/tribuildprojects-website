## What Is Temporary Fencing?

Temporary fencing is a portable fencing system designed to create a secure barrier around construction sites, renovation projects, and other temporary work areas. Unlike permanent fencing, it can be installed quickly, relocated as needed, and removed once the project is complete.

Temporary fencing is commonly used for:

- New home construction
- Home renovations
- Roof replacement projects
- Demolition works
- Commercial construction
- Civil projects
- Landscaping projects
- Vacant land protection

## When Do You Need Temporary Fence Hire?

### New Home Construction

Building a new home involves open work areas, construction materials, and machinery. Temporary fencing helps keep the site secure while protecting workers and the public.

### Home Renovations

Major renovations often involve multiple trades working simultaneously. Temporary fencing helps separate the work zone from occupied areas, improving safety for homeowners and visitors. If you're planning a project, refer to our [complete homeowner guide to residential scaffolding](/blog/residential-scaffolding-sydney-complete-homeowners-guide) for site setup safety guidelines.

### Roof Replacement Projects

Roof replacement projects involve scaffolding, roofing materials, and work at height. Temporary fencing helps prevent unauthorised access and keeps children, pets, and pedestrians away from potential hazards. Be sure to coordinate this with your roofing scaffold setup; learn more in our guide on [scaffolding for roof repairs in Sydney](/blog/do-i-need-scaffolding-for-roof-repairs-in-sydney).

### Demolition Projects

Demolition work creates significant safety risks. Temporary fencing establishes a secure perimeter around the site while protecting surrounding properties and the public.

### Vacant Land

Temporary fencing can also help secure vacant blocks before construction begins, reducing the risk of trespassing and illegal dumping.
