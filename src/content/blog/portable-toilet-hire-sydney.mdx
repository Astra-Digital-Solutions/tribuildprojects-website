## What Is Portable Toilet Hire?

Portable toilet hire provides a temporary toilet facility that can be delivered to a construction or renovation site for the duration of a project.

Instead of relying on the bathroom inside the property, a portable toilet can provide a dedicated facility that is positioned at a suitable location on the site.

Portable toilets can be useful during:

- New home construction
- Home renovations
- Home extensions
- Knockdown and rebuild projects
- Roofing projects
- Residential construction sites
- Long-duration renovation projects
- Projects where existing bathroom facilities are unavailable

The appropriate arrangement depends on the site, project duration, number of workers and available space.

## Where Should a Portable Toilet Be Positioned?

The location of a portable toilet should be considered before delivery.

The best position will depend on the property and site conditions.

Before arranging delivery, consider:

### Site Access

There needs to be suitable access for the delivery vehicle.

Consider the driveway, gate width, access path and any temporary obstructions.

### Ground Conditions

The unit should be positioned on a suitable, stable area.

Avoid placing it where ground conditions could create access or stability problems.

### Worker Access

The location should be reasonably accessible to workers without creating unnecessary movement through the residential property.

### Construction Activities

Consider how the site will change as construction progresses.

A location that works during the initial stage may become inconvenient once materials, scaffolding or other equipment are installed.

### Existing Site Equipment

If the site also has temporary fencing, scaffolding, building materials or other equipment, plan the layout before delivery.

Good site planning can help prevent unnecessary relocation later.
