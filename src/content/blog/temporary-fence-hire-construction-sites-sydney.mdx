## What Is Temporary Construction Fencing?

Temporary construction fencing is a removable fencing system used to establish a temporary boundary around a construction or work area.

Unlike permanent fencing, temporary fencing can be installed for the duration of a project and removed when it is no longer required.

Temporary fencing may be useful for:

- New residential construction
- Home renovations
- Home extensions
- Knockdown and rebuild projects
- Construction sites
- Residential developments
- Landscaping and external works
- Sites requiring temporary access control

The appropriate fencing arrangement depends on the individual property, site layout and construction requirements. For general principles on residential security panels, read our initial [Temporary Fence Hire Sydney guide](/blog/temporary-fence-hire-sydney).

## Why Use Temporary Fencing on a Construction Site?

Construction sites can contain building materials, tools, equipment, scaffolding and areas where construction work is taking place.

Temporary fencing can help establish a clear boundary around the active work area.

For residential projects, this can provide a practical way to separate the construction area from surrounding parts of the property.

Temporary fencing may also help with:

### Defining the Work Area

A clearly defined site boundary can make it easier to identify the area being used for construction activities.

### Managing Site Access

Temporary fencing can be arranged with suitable access points so that workers, contractors and authorised visitors can enter and leave the site.

### Protecting the Construction Area

A temporary boundary can help separate construction activities from areas outside the work zone.

### Supporting Site Organisation

Temporary fencing can form part of the overall site setup alongside scaffolding, building materials, access equipment and other temporary facilities.

The exact purpose and configuration will depend on the individual project.

## When Should Temporary Fencing Be Installed?

The timing of temporary fence installation depends on the construction schedule and the condition of the site.

For many projects, fencing is considered before major construction activity begins.

Planning the fencing early allows the builder or homeowner to consider:

- Site boundaries
- Vehicle access
- Pedestrian access
- Material deliveries
- Existing driveways and gates
- Scaffolding locations
- Building materials
- Portable site facilities
- Future construction activities

Early planning can help prevent the temporary fencing from interfering with other parts of the construction setup.

## Where Should Temporary Fencing Be Positioned?

There is no single fencing layout that works for every construction site.

The position should be planned around the property and the work being carried out.

Before installation, consider:

### Property Boundaries

Identify the area that needs to be separated from the surrounding property.

### Construction Area

Consider where the main construction activities will take place.

### Vehicle Access

Delivery vehicles and construction vehicles may require access to the site.

### Pedestrian Access

Workers and authorised visitors may require dedicated entry points.

### Building Materials

Consider where materials will be delivered and stored.

### Scaffolding

If residential scaffolding is being installed, make sure the fencing layout and scaffold access requirements are considered together.

### Portable Site Facilities

If the project also requires a portable toilet or other temporary facilities, allow suitable space within the overall site layout.

Good planning before installation can make the site easier to organise as construction progresses.
