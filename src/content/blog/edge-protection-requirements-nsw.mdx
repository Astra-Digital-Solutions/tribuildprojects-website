## What Is Edge Protection?

Edge protection is a temporary safety system installed around exposed roof edges and elevated work areas during construction. It helps create a safer work environment by providing a protective barrier while trades carry out roofing, renovation and construction work.

A typical edge protection system includes:

- Guard rails
- Vertical posts
- Support brackets
- Temporary perimeter barriers

These systems are commonly used during residential construction and roofing projects where workers need access around roof edges.

## When Is Edge Protection Used?

Edge protection is commonly used for projects such as:
